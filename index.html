About

One oven, two tables, neighbourhood first.

Started with a single wood-fired oven and two tables. Now a corner bistro where dough ferments 36 hours and veg arrives before sunrise.

Every plate built around fire — char, smoke, coal. No tasting menus, just good food made daily.
